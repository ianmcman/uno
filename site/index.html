    How to play

    • Everyone starts with 7 cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card by colour or by number or symbol.
    • No match, or you'd rather not? Draw one card. If you can play it you may, otherwise keep it and pass.
    • Skip loses the next player's turn. Reverse changes direction (with two players it acts as a skip). Draw Two makes the next player draw two and miss a turn.
    • Wild picks the next colour. Wild Draw Four also makes the next player draw four and miss a turn, but you can only play it with no card of the current colour.
    • When you are down to one card you must call Uno. Tap Uno! before you play your second to last card, or right after. If someone taps Catch before the next player moves, you draw two.
    • The first player to play their last card wins. Points for the cards left in other hands: numbers face value, action cards 20, wilds 50.
